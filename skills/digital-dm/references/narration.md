# Narration

## Defaults
- Clear, concrete, short paragraphs
- End on a direct prompt (what can the PC do *now*)
- Match register to the campaign (tavern plain speech, bridge reports, etc.) without parody

## Do not
- Poetry stacks, symbolic narrator winks, metaphor piles
- Empty technobabble / tautology (“the locked door is still locked”)
- Offer actions impossible from current position, range, or tools
- Fake urgency or meaningless A/B choices
- Dump DM secrets or `[DM]` sections

## Sensible options
Before offering a choice, check: Can they do this *here*, *now*, with what they have?  
If the only honest move is “log it and continue,” say that.

## Combat / rolls
Call for rolls when failure matters. State DC/goal plainly. Track HP and conditions in party files.

## Tone palette
For characterful range (bits + earnest stakes), see [tone-inspirations.md](tone-inspirations.md). Still: plain, sensible, no steering toward comedy or prestige drama.
