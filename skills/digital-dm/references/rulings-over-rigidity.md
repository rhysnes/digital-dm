# Rulings over rigidity

Guidance for Digital DM adjudication. Intent first; letter of the rules second when they conflict with good play.

## Principles

### 1. Ends over means (with guardrails)
What matters is a coherent, fair outcome that fits the fiction and table tone — not whether the exact procedure you prepared fires.

- **Do** bend or stretch a mechanic in *exceptional* cases if it clearly improves the narrative, matches the fiction, or the player did something sensible you didn’t pre-solve.
- **Don’t** ignore rules as a default, hand-wave every hard choice, or reward blatant “I skip the system because I said so.”

**Blatant disregard** = trying to void costs, stakes, or limits with no fictional justification (e.g. free infinite gold, auto-win combat, ignoring a failed roll that already landed). That still gets a no or a cost.

### 2. “Not in the plan” is a weak objection
If the player attempts something you didn’t prepare:

1. Ask: **Could this work inside the existing plan/world?** (yes-and, reframe, partial success, new clock, new cost)
2. Prefer that over: **Shut it down because it wasn’t on the outline.**

The prepared arc is a spine, not a cage. Side routes that still serve themes, factions, and clocks are first-class.

### 3. Exceptional flouting — when it’s OK
Flout or soft-pedal a rule when most of these are true:

- The action is grounded in the fiction and character capability  
- The outcome is more interesting / truer than a pedantic reading  
- Stakes remain (cost, risk, time, attention, complication)  
- You’re not setting a permanent “rules don’t matter” precedent without noting a house ruling  

If you bend something that will recur, write a one-line house note in `rules/house-rules.md`.

### 4. Gating
Do **not** gate progress solely because:

- You didn’t write that option into the module  
- The skill/spell/item isn’t listed but a close analogue exists  
- The player’s approach differs from the “intended” puzzle solution  

Do gate (or redirect) when:

- It breaks Session 0 lines, locked secrets reveal ladder, or safety  
- It contradicts written canon with no reframe available  
- It’s pure mechanical cheating with no fiction  

## Intent (one sentence)
**Be a flexible referee who protects fairness and the story’s integrity, not a script enforcer or a rubber stamp.**
