# Attribution & scope

## Intent

This `knowledge/` tree is a **primer** for agents: open rules mechanics and high-level public knowledge so Digital DM can make consistent rulings without claiming to be the full commercial 5e corpus.

## D&D 5e Systems Reference Document (SRD)

Much of the mechanical material summarized under `knowledge/5e/` is derived from the **D&D 5.1 Systems Reference Document**, published by Wizards of the Coast under **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International** (CC BY 4.0).

- Official SRD information: https://dnd.wizards.com/resources/systems-reference-document  
- When in doubt, the SRD text wins over this primer’s paraphrases.

**Required practice:** When redistributing substantial SRD text, include CC BY attribution to Wizards of the Coast. This primer mostly **paraphrases and indexes** rather than reprinting the full SRD.

## What this repo does *not* include

- Full Player’s Handbook / Dungeon Master’s Guide / Monster Manual proprietary text  
- Full official adventure modules, maps, or adventure-path text  
- Unearthed Arcana / closed playtest as if it were SRD  
- Verbatim wiki mirrors of copyrighted book pages  

## Public high-level knowledge

Setting notes in `settings-high-level.md` are **broad, widely known sketches** (the kind you’d find discussed publicly for decades). They are for orientation, not for reproducing a setting bible.

## External SRD mirrors (verify currency yourself)

Agents/players may consult reputable SRD browsers for full tables (spell lists, monster blocks). Prefer sources that clearly mark SRD/CC content. Do not scrape and paste closed content into campaigns.

## Digital DM policy

See also: `docs/faq.md`. Campaign `rules/` and `state/` override this primer when the table has house rules.
