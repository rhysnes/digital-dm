## Not in this compendium (by design)
- Downtime / crafting / stronghold systems  
- Default world gazetteer (each campaign invents setting)

## Design rules
- Math targets 5e bounded accuracy (see `../loose-5e.md`)
- Names and lore are original; mechanics may *resemble* open SRD patterns without reprinting closed text
- Sci-fi tables: reskin damage types, names, and “magic” as tech/psi per campaign `rules/`
- If a feature conflicts with Session 0 (e.g. no magic), ban the relevant classes/spells or convert to devices
