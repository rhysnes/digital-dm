# Vehicles (homebrew)

Proficiency with **vehicles (land / water / space)** adds proficiency bonus to checks to control them under stress.

## Mounts & draft (fantasy)

| Mastiff | 25 gp | 40 ft | — |

**Cart** 15 gp · **Wagon** 35 gp · **Carriage** 100 gp · **Sled** 20 gp  

Overland: mount + rider ≈ travel pace of mounted creature; wagons usually **normal** pace on roads.

| Sailing ship | 10,000 gp | 2 mph | 20+ | serious cargo |
| War galley | 30,000 gp | 2.5 mph | 80+ | troops |

**Checks:** Navigation (Wis + navigator’s tools), storm Piloting (vehicles water), repairs (carpenter’s tools).  
**Combat:** treat as objects with HP/AC (rowboat AC 11 HP 50; sailing ship AC 15 HP 300 — DM scale). Called shots to sails/rudder impose disadv. or speed 0.

## Spacecraft (sci-fi reskin layer)

Use when campaign is ship-based. Keep fiction first; don’t simulate full engineering unless fun.

| Class | Role | Notes |
|-------|------|-------|
| Shuttle | Short hops | 1 pilot; 4–8 passengers; fragile |
| Freighter | Cargo | Slow; room for trouble |
| Explorer (PC ship) | Survey | Sensors, one shuttle, modest guns |
| Patrol cutter | Fight | Faster, thinner cargo |

### Soft ship combat (optional)
Each round: **Helm** (Dex/vehicles) · **Tactical** (Int/Attack) · **Engineering** (Int/tools)  
- Success Helm: gain position (+1 next attack or escape)  
- Tactical hit: deal 1 “system box” or board  
- Engineering: clear a box / restore shields  

Ship HP as **system boxes** (6–12). At 0: dead in space, not always destroyed.

**Boarding:** use normal combat on decks.

## Chases
Both sides roll vehicles/Athletics each round; accumulate successes to escape or catch (3–5). Terrain complications every round (DC 12–18).
