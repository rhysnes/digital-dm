# Spell Compendium (homebrew)

All spells below are **original names/effects** for this toolkit. Casting times are 1 action unless noted. Range/self/touch as listed. Duration Instantaneous unless noted. Concentration = C.

**Attack** = spell attack · **Save** = listed ability vs your spell save DC.

Sci-fi reskin: rename freely; force ≈ directed energy; radiant ≈ hard light; necrotic ≈ entropy/void.

---

## Cantrips

**Ember Needle** — Evocation · V,S · 60 ft · Instant  
Ranged spell attack; 1d8 fire (2d8 at 5th, 3d8 at 11th, 4d8 at 17th).

**Force Needle** — Evocation · V,S · 120 ft · Instant  
Ranged spell attack; 1d10 force (scales as Ember Needle).

**Chill Thread** — Necromancy · V,S · 60 ft · Instant  
Ranged spell attack; 1d8 cold; target’s speed −10 ft until your next turn end (scales d8).

**Guidance Pulse** — Divination · V,S · Touch · C, 1 min  
Ally adds 1d4 to one ability check before duration ends.

**Stabilize** — Necromancy · V,S · Touch · Instant  
Stabilize a living creature at 0 HP. (Sci-fi: emergency med-gel.)

**Light Bead** — Evocation · V,M · Touch · 1 hour  
Object sheds bright light 20 ft / dim 20 ft. End as action. One bead at a time.

**Prestidigit** — Transmutation · V,S · 10 ft · 1 hour  
Minor sensory trick, clean/soil, or warm/cool; harmless.

**Shocking Grasp** — Evocation · V,S · Touch · Instant  
Melee spell attack; advantage if target wears metal armor; 1d8 lightning (scales); target can’t take reactions until its next turn start.

**Mending Flicker** — Transmutation · V,S · Touch · Instant  
Repair a single break in an object ≤ 1 ft across (no magic/tech function restore if DM says so).

**Message Spark** — Transmutation · V,S · 120 ft · 1 round  
Whisper to one creature; they can reply. Others don’t hear.

## 1st level

**Shard Volley** — Evocation · V,S · 120 ft · Instant  
Three darts; each ranged spell attack 1d4+1 force. Darts can hit one or split targets. Higher slots: +1 dart / slot above 1st. (Missile analogue.)

**Ablative Skin** — Abjuration · V,S · Touch · C, 1 hour? No — **8 hours** (not C)  
Target’s base AC becomes 13 + Dex if not wearing armor. Ends if armor donned.

**Repair Field** — Evocation · V,S · Touch · Instant  
Creature regains 1d8 + casting mod HP. Higher slots: +1d8 / slot.

**Fear Spike** — Illusion · V,S · 30 ft · C, 1 min  
Wis save or frightened of you until duration ends; repeat save each turn end.

**Fog Bank** — Conjuration · V,S · 120 ft · C, 1 hour  
20-ft-radius sphere heavily obscures. Wind can disperse (DM).

**Detect Anomaly** — Divination · V,S · Self · C, 10 min  
Sense magic **or** unusual tech signatures (campaign pick) within 30 ft; not through total cover.

**Jump Coil** — Transmutation · V,S · Touch · 1 min  
Triple jump distance.

**Hexbind** — Enchantment · V,S · 90 ft · C, 1 hour  
Choose a creature; it has disadvantage on checks with one ability of your choice. If it drops to 0 HP, use a bonus action to move Hexbind to another within 30 ft.

**Shield Flutter** — Abjuration · V,S · Self · 1 reaction (when hit) · 1 round  
+5 AC until start of your next turn, including against triggering attack; auto-stop *Shard Volley* if applicable.

**Sleep Soft** — Enchantment · V,S,M · 90 ft · 1 min  
Roll 5d8; creatures in 20-ft radius (lowest HP first) fall unconscious if HP ≤ remaining pool. Higher slots +2d8. Wakes on damage or ally action.

**Grease Slick** — Conjuration · V,S · 60 ft · 1 min  
10-ft square difficult; enter/start turn Dex save or prone.

**Command Word** — Enchantment · V · 60 ft · 1 round  
One-word command (Approach, Drop, Flee, Grovel, Halt). Wis save negates. Undead/machines immune unless DM allows.
