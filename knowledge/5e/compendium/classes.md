# Classes (homebrew, levels 1–10)

Hit points, proficiency bonus, and multiclassing follow open 5e procedures (`../character-basics.md`, `../loose-5e.md`). Spellcasters use the **Compendium spell list** in `spells.md`.

**Spell save DC** = `8 + proficiency + casting ability mod`  
**Spell attack** = `proficiency + casting ability mod`

---

## Vanguard (martial tank/striker)
**Hit die** d10 · **Saves** Str, Con · **Armor** all armor, shields · **Weapons** simple + martial  
**Skills:** pick 2 from Athletics, Intimidation, Perception, Survival, Insight, Animal Handling  

| Level | Features |
|-------|----------|
| 1 | Fighting Style*; Second Wind (bonus action: 1d10+level HP, 1/short rest) |
| 2 | Action Surge (1/short rest) |
| 3 | Archetype: **Bulwark** (Protection reaction: impose disadvantage on an attack vs adjacent ally) or **Raider** (+1d6 damage 1/turn if you moved 10+ ft toward target) |
| 5 | Extra Attack |
| 6 | Ability Score Improvement (+2 total) |
| 7 | Archetype upgrade: Bulwark +1 AC in heavy armor; Raider ignore nonmagical difficult terrain when charging |
| 9 | Indomitable (reroll a save, 1/long rest) |
| 10 | Extra Attack (still 2) · ASI |

\*Styles: Defense (+1 AC armor), Dueling (+2 damage one-handed), Great Weapon (reroll 1–2 on damage die), Archery (+2 ranged attacks), Protection (as Bulwark reaction if not taken).

## Shadow (skill striker)
**Hit die** d8 · **Saves** Dex, Int · **Armor** light · **Weapons** simple + hand crossbow, shortsword, rapier, shortbow  
**Skills:** pick 4 from Acrobatics, Athletics, Deception, Insight, Intimidation, Investigation, Perception, Performance, Persuasion, Sleight of Hand, Stealth  

| Level | Features |
|-------|----------|
| 1 | Expertise (×2 skills); Sneak Attack 1d6; Thieves’ Cant (or Crew Cipher in sci-fi) |
| 2 | Cunning Action (Dash/Disengage/Hide as bonus action) |
| 3 | Archetype: **Cutpurse** (bonus proficiency thieves’ tools; Fast Hands) or **Assassin** (advantage on initiative if not surprised; crits on surprised foes) · Sneak 2d6 |
| 5 | Uncanny Dodge; Sneak 3d6 |
| 6 | Expertise ×2 more |
| 7 | Evasion; Sneak 4d6 |
| 9 | Archetype: Cutpurse Reliable Talent (treat d20≤9 as 10 on proficient checks); Assassin infiltrate identity 1/long rest · Sneak 5d6 |
| 10 | ASI · Sneak 5d6 |

Sneak Attack: 1/turn when you hit with finesse/ranged and have advantage, **or** an non-incapacitated ally is within 5 ft of the target and you don’t have disadvantage.

## Channeler (full caster — prepared)
**Hit die** d6 · **Saves** Int, Wis · **Armor** none · **Weapons** dagger, dart, sling, quarterstaff, light crossbow  
**Skills:** pick 2 from Arcana/Science, History, Insight, Investigation, Medicine, Religion  
**Casting ability:** Intelligence · **Cantrips known:** 3 → 4 (4th) → 5 (10th)

## Adept (ki / focus martial)
**Hit die** d8 · **Saves** Str, Dex · **Armor** none · **Weapons** simple + shortsword  
**Skills:** pick 2 from Acrobatics, Athletics, History, Insight, Religion, Stealth  
**Unarmored Defense:** AC = 10 + Dex + Wis  
**Focus points** = level (min 1); recharge short rest  

| Level | Features |
|-------|----------|
| 1 | Martial Arts (Dex for unarmed/shortsword; unarmed 1d4→1d6 at 5; bonus unarmed when you Attack with monk weapon/unarmed) |
| 2 | Focus Arts: Flurry (1 focus: 2 bonus unarmed), Patient Defense (1: Dodge bonus), Step (1: Disengage+Dash bonus) |
| 3 | Tradition **Open Hand** (Flurry knock prone or push 15 ft, Str save) or **Shadow** (Darkvision 60; silence step) |
| 5 | Extra Attack; Stunning Strike (1 focus: Con save or stunned until your next turn end) |
| 6 | Ki-Empowered: unarmed count as magical (or “hardened field”) |
| 10 | ASI · Purity (immune disease/poison) |

**Skills:** pick any 3 · **Expertise** ×2 at 1st, ×2 at 6th  
**Casting:** Charisma · known full-caster pace but **magical secrets** limited — use Bard-like slots as Channeler table · spells from Compendium  

| Level | Features |
|-------|----------|
| 1 | Spellcasting (full known caster); Bardic Push (bonus: 1d6 to an ally’s check/attack within 60 ft, uses = Cha mod/long rest) |
| 2 | Jack of All Trades (+half prof to untrained checks); Song of Rest (+1d6 HP on short rest Hit Dice) |
| 3 | College: **Spy** (Stealth expertise if not taken; mimic voices) or **Diplomat** (Persuasion: reroll 1/long rest) |
| 5 | Font: Regain Push uses on short rest; Push die d8 |
| 6 | Countercharm (advantage vs fright/charm for allies hearing you) |
| 10 | Push die d10 · ASI · Magical Secret (learn 1 spell from any list) |

## Multiclass (loose)
Need 13 in the new class’s key ability (Str/Dex Vanguard/Shadow/Adept; Int Channeler; Wis Devoted/Stalker; Cha Pactbound/Face). Slots combine as open 5e multiclass caster table if used — or forbid multiclass for simplicity.
