# Traps & hazards (homebrew)

## Detecting & disarming
- **Passive Perception** notices obvious traps (DC ≤ passive).  
- Active **Investigation** finds triggers; **thieves’ tools** / Arcana / appropriate tool to disarm (usually same DC).  
- Failure by 5+ on disarm often triggers.

|------|------------------|---------|--------|
| **Pit (10 ft)** | 12 / — | Floor | Dex DC 13; 1d6 fall; climb DC 12 out |
| **Pit (20 ft) spiked** | 15 / 15 | Floor | Dex DC 15; 2d6 fall + 2d6 pierce |
| **Poison dart** | 15 / 15 | Pressure plate | +5 attack; 1d4 + Nettle Oil |
| **Swinging blade** | 15 / 15 | Tripwire | +6; 2d8 slash; corridor |
| **Falling net** | 12 / 12 | Tripwire | Dex DC 12 or restrained; Str DC 12 escape; AC 10 HP 5 |
| **Collapsing roof** | 16 / 18 | Lever / weight | Dex DC 15; 4d10 B in 10-ft square (half) |
| **Flame jet** | 15 / 15 | Pressure | Dex DC 14; 3d6 fire (half); 1/round while pressed |
| **Sleep gas** | 14 / 16 | Open chest | Con DC 13; Sleep Soft pool 5d8 in 10-ft |
| **Rune shock** | 16 / 16 (Arcana) | Touch glyph | Dex DC 14; 3d8 lightning; alert alarm |
| **Gravity shear (sci-fi)** | 15 / 17 | Corridor | Str DC 14 or prone + 2d6 force; difficult terrain |

**Complex traps:** initiative; act each round until disabled (HP or 3 successful disarm checks).

## Falling
1d6 bludgeoning / 10 ft (max 20d6). Dex DC 15 half if can grab surface mid-fall (DM).

## Magical / anomalous zones
**Wild surge zone:** cast spell → roll d6: 1 backfire half damage self; 2–5 normal; 6 doubled effect once.  
**Silence field:** as Silence Bell.  
**Antimagic pocket:** spells fail; magic items go dormant (rare — use sparingly).
