# Backgrounds (homebrew)

Each grants: **2 skill proficiencies**, **tool and/or language**, a **feature**, and suggested equipment (or 15 gp + tools).

---

### Frontier Scout
**Skills** Perception, Survival · **Tools** navigator’s tools *or* herbalism kit · **Lang** one  
**Feature — Known Ground:** In wilderness you know, you find food/water for 6 and avoid natural hazards with a travel lead.  
**Gear:** staff, hunting trap, map case, traveler’s clothes, 10 gp

### Dockworker / Deckhand
**Skills** Athletics, Perception · **Tools** vehicles (water or space) · **Lang** one  
**Feature — Port Gossip:** You know how to find cheap berths, cargo rumors, and who takes bribes in any port you’ve worked.  
**Gear:** crowbar, work clothes, 15 gp

### Archivist
**Skills** History, Investigation · **Tools** calligrapher’s supplies · **Lang** two  
**Feature — Library Access:** Scholars and clerks treat you as peer; you get into ordinary archives with a letter of introduction.  
**Gear:** ink, pen, 10 sheets, reference book, robe, 10 gp

### Street Operator
**Skills** Deception, Stealth · **Tools** thieves’ tools · **Lang** thieves’ cant / crew cipher  
**Feature — Black Market Nod:** You can find a fence or info broker in any city within a day.  
**Gear:** crowbar, dark clothes, thieves’ tools, 15 gp

### Medicae
**Skills** Medicine, Insight · **Tools** herbalism kit *or* healer’s kit proficiency  
**Feature — Triage Authority:** In crises, people let you through crowds; you can request basic medical supplies once per town.  
**Gear:** healer’s kit, herbalism kit, scrub clothes, 10 gp

### Faithful / Chaplain
**Skills** Insight, Religion · **Lang** two · **Focus** holy symbol / oath token  
**Feature — Shelter of the Order:** Temples/chapels of your tradition give modest lodging and information.  
**Gear:** symbol, prayer book, incense, common clothes, 15 gp

### Merchant Factor
**Skills** Insight, Persuasion · **Tools** one gaming set *or* navigator’s · **Lang** two  
**Feature — Credit Line:** Once per settlement, borrow up to 25 gp locally if you leave collateral/name (DM tracks debt).  
**Gear:** abacus, ledger, fine clothes, 25 gp

### Soldier / Marine
**Skills** Athletics, Intimidation · **Tools** one gaming set · vehicles (land) *or* (space)  
**Feature — Rank Recognition:** Other military recognize your former unit; you can get audience with NCOs.  
**Gear:** rank insignia, trophy, gaming set, common clothes, 10 gp

### Entertainer
**Skills** Acrobatics, Performance · **Tools** one instrument · **Lang** one  
**Feature — By Popular Demand:** You find free lodging in exchange for a performance most nights.  
**Gear:** instrument, costume, 15 gp

### Artisan
**Skills** Insight, Persuasion · **Tools** one artisan’s tools · **Lang** one  
**Feature — Guild Ties:** Fellow artisans give workshop access and apprenticeship gossip.  
**Gear:** tools, letter of introduction, traveler’s clothes, 15 gp

### Hermit / Field Researcher
**Skills** Medicine, Religion *or* Nature · **Tools** herbalism kit · **Lang** one  
**Feature — Discovery:** You hold one secret or sample useful to the campaign (define with DM).  
**Gear:** scroll case of notes, winter blanket, herbalism kit, common clothes, 5 gp

### Noble / Officer Scion
**Skills** History, Persuasion · **Tools** one gaming set · **Lang** one  
**Feature — Position:** Your name opens doors among elites; servants assume you belong.  
**Gear:** fine clothes, signet, pedigree scroll, 25 gp

### Criminal (Syndicate)
**Skills** Deception, Stealth · **Tools** thieves’ tools · gaming set · **Lang** one  
**Feature — Bad Reputation:** People avoid crossing you casually; contacts among crooks.  
**Gear:** crowbar, dark clothes, 15 gp

### Sailor / Spacer
**Skills** Athletics, Perception · **Tools** navigator’s tools · vehicles (water/space)  
**Feature — Ship’s Passage:** Secure free passage on a freighter for yourself in return for work (companions pay half).  
**Gear:** belaying pin/baton, silk rope 50 ft, lucky charm, common clothes, 10 gp

### Urchin
**Skills** Sleight of Hand, Stealth · **Tools** disguise kit · thieves’ tools  
**Feature — City Secrets:** Know shortcuts; move through your home city at double travel pace when unencumbered.  
**Gear:** small knife, map of city, pet mouse, common clothes, 10 gp
