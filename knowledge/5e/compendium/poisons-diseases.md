# Poisons & diseases (homebrew)

## Poisons

**Applying:** action (weapon) or bonus if feature allows. Wound poisons need a damaging hit. Ingested need swallowed. Inhaled: enter cloud / fail save when exposed. Contact: touch.

| Poison | Type | Price | Save | Effect |
|--------|------|-------|------|--------|
| **Nettle Oil** | Wound | 50 gp | Con DC 11 | 1d4 poison; poisoned until end of its next turn |
| **Serpent Ichor** | Wound | 150 gp | Con DC 13 | 3d6 poison (half on success); poisoned 1 min (save end of turn) |
| **Midnight Tears** | Ingested | 1,000 gp | Con DC 17 | No immediate effect; at midnight: 9d6 poison (half) |
| **Torpor** | Ingested | 400 gp | Con DC 15 | Poisoned + incapacitated 1d6 hours (save ends each hour after first) |
| **Burning Spore** | Inhaled | 200 gp | Con DC 14 | 15-ft cube cloud 1 min; enter/start: 2d6 poison + poisoned 1 min |
| **Pale Toxin** | Contact | 300 gp | Con DC 15 | 4d6 poison; if fail by 5+, paralyzed 1 min (save end of turn) |
| **Truth Serum Soft** | Ingested | 100 gp | Con DC 12 | Disadvantage on Deception 10 min; Charm-adjacent honesty (DM) |

**Crafting:** not covered here (deferred). Buy or loot only unless campaign adds crafting.

**Resistance / immunity:** creatures immune to poison ignore; antitoxin helps saves.

## Diseases

Track onset and saves. Medicine checks can grant advantage on a save (DM).

### Spreading Cough
**Transmission** inhaled, close quarters 24 hrs  
**Onset** 1d4 days · **Save** Con DC 12 daily  
**Effect** Disadvantage on Str/Dex checks & attacks while fevered; fail 3 saves → exhaustion +1; succeed 3 → cured.

### Crypt Rot
**Transmission** wound from undead · **Onset** 1 day · **Save** Con DC 14 daily  
**Effect** HP max −1d6 per failed save; at 0 max → die. 3 successes cure; *Repair* spells don’t restore max until cured.

### Mindhaze
**Transmission** psychic parasite / bad air · **Onset** 1d6 hours · **Save** Wis DC 13 after each long rest  
**Effect** Disadvantage on Int/Wis checks; hallucinations (DM). 3 successes cure; fail 3 → short-term madness 1 day (incapable of reliable plans).

### Blightbite
**Transmission** contact with blight water · **Onset** 1 hour · **Save** Con DC 15 once after onset  
**Effect** Fail: poisoned until cured by lesser restoration analogue (**Purge Toxin** — treat as 2nd-level Devoted/Channeler spell: end poison/disease) or 3 successful daily saves.

### Nanite Fever (sci-fi)
As Spreading Cough but Int saves instead of Con once per day; fail → disadvantage on tool checks; “cured” by medbay long rest + Medicine DC 15.
