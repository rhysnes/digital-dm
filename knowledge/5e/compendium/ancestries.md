# Ancestries (homebrew)

Pick one. Ability bumps apply at character creation. Size Medium unless noted. Speed 30 ft unless noted.

---

## Human (Versatile)
- **Ability:** +1 to all six, **or** +2 to one and +1 to another (choose at creation)
- **Skill:** proficiency in one skill of your choice
- **Feat/Edge:** one **Origin Edge** (see bottom) *or* +1 skill proficiency
- **Languages:** Common + one

## Elf (Ashvein)
Long-lived, keen-sensed.  
- **Ability:** +2 Dex, +1 Int or Wis  
- **Darkvision** 60 ft  
- **Fey Ancestry:** advantage on saves vs charmed; magic can’t put you to sleep  
- **Trance:** 4 hours of semi-conscious rest counts as a long rest for you (allies still need 8)  
- **Skill:** Perception proficiency  
- **Languages:** Common, Elvish

## Dwarf (Stonekin)
- **Ability:** +2 Con, +1 Wis or Str  
- **Speed** 25 ft (not reduced by heavy armor)  
- **Darkvision** 60 ft  
- **Resilience:** resistance to poison damage; advantage on saves vs poison  
- **Tool:** smith, brewer, or mason tools  
- **Languages:** Common, Dwarvish

## Halfling (Hearthfoot)
- **Ability:** +2 Dex, +1 Cha  
- **Size** Small · **Speed** 25 ft  
- **Lucky:** when you roll a 1 on a d20 for attack/check/save, reroll once and use the new roll  
- **Brave:** advantage on saves vs frightened  
- **Nimble:** move through the space of any creature larger than you  
- **Languages:** Common, Halfling

## Orckin
- **Ability:** +2 Str, +1 Con  
- **Darkvision** 60 ft  
- **Relentless (1/long rest):** if you’d drop to 0 HP but not die outright, drop to 1 HP instead  
- **Menacing:** Intimidation proficiency  
- **Languages:** Common, Orkish

## Dragonblood
- **Ability:** +2 Str or Cha, +1 to the other  
- **Draconic Resistance:** choose acid, cold, fire, lightning, or poison — resistance to that damage  
- **Breath (1/short rest):** 15-ft cone or 30-ft line (your choice at creation); Con save DC `8 + prof + Con or Cha`; 2d6 damage of your element (3d6 at 6th, 4d6 at 11th, 5d6 at 16th)  
- **Languages:** Common, Draconic

## Gnome (Spark) 
- **Ability:** +2 Int, +1 Dex or Con  
- **Size** Small · **Speed** 25 ft  
- **Darkvision** 60 ft  
- **Cunning:** advantage on Int, Wis, Cha saves vs magic  
- **Tinker Craft:** know one cantrip from the Compendium (Int spellcasting)  
- **Languages:** Common, Gnomish

## Tiefling (Cinderborn)
- **Ability:** +2 Cha, +1 Int  
- **Darkvision** 60 ft  
- **Hellish Resistance:** fire resistance  
- **Legacy:** know cantrip *Ember Needle*; at 3rd *Shadow Step* 1/long rest; at 5th *Ash Veil* 1/long rest (Cha)  
- **Languages:** Common, Infernal

## Changeling (Mask)
- **Ability:** +2 Cha, +1 Dex or Int  
- **Shapechange (action):** alter appearance and voice; stats unchanged; clothing doesn’t resize  
- **Insightful:** Insight proficiency  
- **Languages:** Common + two

## Warforged-analogue: **Constructed**
Living construct / android-friendly.  
- **Ability:** +2 Con, +1 any other  
- **Constructed Resilience:** advantage vs poison; resistance to poison; don’t need to eat, drink, or breathe; immune to disease  
- **Integrated Protection:** AC 13 + Dex while unarmored (or use armor)  
- **Sentry’s Rest:** 6 hours inactive = long rest  
- **Languages:** Common + one

## Planetouched (Aether)
- **Ability:** +2 Wis or Cha, +1 Con  
- **Darkvision** 60 ft  
- **Aether Step (1/short rest):** teleport up to 15 ft to a seen spot as a bonus action  
- **Languages:** Common + Celestial or Void Speech

---

## Origin Edges (pick if Human Versatile grants one)
- **Toughened:** +1 HP per level  
- **Skilled:** proficiency in two skills  
- **Alert Start:** +2 initiative  
- **Fleet:** +5 ft speed  
- **Savvy:** proficiency in one tool + one language
