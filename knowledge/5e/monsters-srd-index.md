# SRD monsters — how to use

## Policy

Use **SRD creature statistics** for open play. Do not paste closed Monster Manual-only creatures’ full blocks into the repo. If a player wants a closed monster, they can supply the book text at the table, or you substitute an SRD analogue.

## Famous SRD-friendly names (examples)

Many “iconic” creatures appear in the SRD (exact list depends on SRD version). Examples commonly available in 5.1-style SRD sets include staples such as: goblin, orc, skeleton, zombie, wolf, brown bear, bandit, guard, veteran, mage, adult dragon entries of certain colors, gelatinous cube, owlbear, etc.

**Agent practice:** when you need a block, fetch from a current **SRD source** (see `../ATTRIBUTION.md`) or approximate with a simple custom stat line:

```
Name — AC X, HP Y, Speed Z
Attack +N (XdY+Z damage) · Save DC N · Traits in one line
```

## Building a simple foe (loose)

| Tier | AC | HP ballpark (solo PC adjust down) | Attack bonus |
|------|----|-----------------------------------|--------------|
| Low (1–4) | 12–15 | 10–40 | +3 to +5 |
| Mid (5–10) | 14–17 | 50–150 | +5 to +8 |
| High (11+) | 16–19 | 150+ | +8+ |

Prefer fewer special actions over six legendary paragraphs.

## Legends & lairs

Legendary actions / lair actions exist in SRD for some dragons and similar. Use sparingly for solo PC games.
