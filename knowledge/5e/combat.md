# Combat (primer)

## Surprise & initiative

- Surprised creatures can’t move/act on their first turn and can’t take reactions until that turn ends (SRD surprise rules).  
- Initiative: `d20 + Dexterity modifier` (ties → Dex score or DM call).  
- Combat rounds ≈ 6 seconds; turns in initiative order.

## On your turn (typical)

You can move up to your speed and take **one action**. Possible extras: **bonus action** (only if a feature/spell grants one), **reaction** (once per round, when trigger occurs), **free interaction** (draw a sword, open a door — DM adjudicates).

### Common actions
- Attack (one or more attacks if Extra Attack, etc.)
- Cast a spell
- Dash, Disengage, Dodge
- Help, Hide, Ready, Search, Use an Object

**Opportunity attack:** reaction when a hostile you can see leaves your reach (unless Disengage, etc.).

## Cover (summary)

| Cover | Effect |
|-------|--------|
| Half | +2 AC and DEX saves |
| Three-quarters | +5 AC and DEX saves |
| Total | Can’t be targeted directly |

## Grapple / shove (contests)

Athletics vs Athletics or Acrobatics (target’s choice). Grappled → speed 0. Shove prone or push away (5 feet) on success.

## Hit points & dying

- At 0 HP: unconscious and making **death saving throws** on your turn: d20 — 10+ success, 1–9 fail; 3 fails → die; 3 successes → stable at 0 HP. Natural 1 = two fails; natural 20 → regain 1 HP.  
- Damage at 0 HP: usually 1 fail (or instant death if leftover damage ≥ HP maximum).  
- **Healing** at 0 wakes the creature with that many HP.

## Temporary HP

Buffer that absorbs damage first; doesn’t stack with itself (take the nicer amount); isn’t “real” HP for healing math.

## Mounted / underwater / etc.

Use SRD special rules when those scenes matter; otherwise keep it simple and cinematic under “5e loose.”
