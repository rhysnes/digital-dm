# Settings — high-level public knowledge

Orientation only. Not setting bibles. Prefer **original settings** (Digital DM default) unless the player chooses a known world.

## Forgotten Realms (broad)

Sword-and-sorcery continent **Faerûn** is the default “medieval fantasy with lots of gods and dungeons” backdrop in public conversation: Waterdeep, Baldur’s Gate, Sword Coast, etc. Use only if the player opts in.

## Greyhawk

Older “classic dungeon fantasy” world; city of Greyhawk as hub. Publicly associated with early D&D tone.

## Eberron

Pulp noir + magic-as-tech (lightning rail, warforged, dragonmarks). Good reference when a table wants pulp intrigue rather than Tolkien-pastoral.

## Dragonlance / Dark Sun / Ravenloft / Planescape / Spelljammer

Public one-liners: dragons & war; harsh desert survival; gothic horror domains; philosophy across planes; ships between worlds. Use as **mood references**, not as copied metaplot.

## Modern / sci-fi tables

5e mechanics can drive sci-fi with reskins (laser rifle ≈ martial weapon, “psi” ≈ spells if allowed). Clarke’s-law campaigns may forbid the Spellcasting feature entirely and use devices — see campaign rules.

## Agent rule

If the campaign is original, **do not** drag in Realms gods, Elminster, or module plots unless the player asks.
