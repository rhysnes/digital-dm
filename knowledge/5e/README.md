## Core math (always)

- **d20 + modifiers** vs target number (AC, DC, or contested roll)
- **Proficiency Bonus** by level (SRD): L1–4 **+2**; L5–8 **+3**; L9–12 **+4**; L13–16 **+5**; L17–20 **+6**
- **Ability modifier** = (score − 10) ÷ 2, round down
- **Advantage:** roll 2d20, take higher · **Disadvantage:** take lower · they cancel to a normal roll

## Bounded accuracy (public design intent)

5e keeps bonuses relatively flat so low-level threats can still matter and high-level play doesn’t require astronomical numbers. Prefer DCs in the teens, not 30s, for ordinary heroic tasks.
