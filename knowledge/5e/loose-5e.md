# “5e loose” — Digital DM default

When Session 0 says **D&D 5e (loose)**:

## Keep
- d20 + mod vs AC/DC  
- Advantage/disadvantage  
- Concentration, conditions, death saves  
- Action economy (action / bonus / reaction / move)  
- Bounded DCs (mostly 10–20)  

## Relax
- Exact encumbrance, ammo, and lifestyle expenses until they matter  
- Exact jumping/carrying formulas — ballpark  
- Compulsory XP accounting — prefer milestones  
- Rules-lawyering mid-scene — note it, resolve after the beat if needed  

## Always
- Fiction first  
- Sensible options only  
- Write rulings that will recur into `rules/house-rules.md`  

## Spells & subclasses

If it’s not in the SRD and the player can’t cite their book, offer an SRD spell/feature or a small homebrew with clear limits.
