# Core loop

## When to roll

Roll when: outcome uncertain **and** failure is interesting.  
Don’t roll: pure flavor, automatic success/failure, or no stakes.

## Ability check

`d20 + ability modifier + proficiency (if proficient) + other modifiers` vs **DC**.

Contested: both sides roll; higher wins (ties → status quo or DM call).

## Saving throw

`d20 + ability modifier + proficiency (if proficient in that save)` vs **DC**.  
Common: DEX (area effects), CON (poison/concentration), WIS (many mental effects).

## Attack roll

`d20 + ability modifier + proficiency (if proficient with the weapon/spell) + other mods` vs **Armor Class**.

- **Melee weapons:** usually Strength (finesse → Str or Dex)  
- **Ranged weapons:** usually Dexterity  
- **Spell attacks:** often spellcasting ability  

**Critical hit:** natural 20 on the attack die → roll damage dice twice (modifiers once).  
**Critical miss:** natural 1 on attack misses (common table practice; confirm if using “fumble” house rules — SRD doesn’t require silly fumbles).

## Damage

Roll weapon/spell damage dice + relevant modifiers. Subtract from HP. Resistance = half; vulnerability = double; immunity = none (after other rules).

## Advantage / disadvantage

From circumstances, features, conditions (e.g. blinded → attacks vs you have advantage; your attacks have disadvantage). One of each cancels to normal—don’t stack multiple advantages into more dice.
