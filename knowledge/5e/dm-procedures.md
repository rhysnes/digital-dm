# DM procedures (public practice)

These are **adjudication habits** aligned with open 5e design talk and SRD math — not a reprint of the full DMG.

## Fiction first

1. Player states goal and approach  
2. DM decides: automatic / impossible / roll  
3. Set DC or contested check  
4. Narrate outcome; apply rules after the fiction is clear  

## Fail forward

On failure, advance time, clocks, or cost — avoid “nothing happens” unless that’s the point.

## Encounter difficulty (rule of thumb)

Use CR and XP budgets from **SRD/open calculators** when you need precision. For loose play:

- One monster of CR ≈ party level → often a decent single fight for 3–5 PCs (very approximate)  
- Multiple monsters: action economy usually hurts PCs more than raw HP  
- Solo PC campaigns: cut enemy numbers, use companion NPC budgets, or lower HP — don’t run “four player” deadly fights unchanged  

Always tune to the table’s fun and the campaign’s tone.

## Treasure & magic items

SRD includes some items. Closed books have the famous named loot. For original campaigns: invent items with clear attunement, charges, and rarity tags; keep power on-curve (bounded accuracy).

## XP vs milestone

State the mode in `state/campaign.md`. Milestone fits arc-based Digital DM play.

## Saying no

Short no + alternate path. Never no because of unstated secret gotchas.

## Safety

Lines/veils from Session 0 override “what the monster would do.”
