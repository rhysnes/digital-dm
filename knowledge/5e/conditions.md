# Conditions (SRD summary)

Paraphrased for quick adjudication. Full definitions: SRD.

| Condition | Snapshot |
|-----------|----------|
| **Blinded** | Can’t see; auto-fail sight checks; attacks vs you advantage; your attacks disadvantage |
| **Charmed** | Can’t attack charmer; charmer has advantage on social checks to influence you |
| **Deafened** | Can’t hear; auto-fail hearing checks |
| **Frightened** | Disadvantage on checks/attacks while source in sight; can’t willingly move closer |
| **Grappled** | Speed 0; ends if grappler incapacitated or you escape |
| **Incapacitated** | No actions/reactions |
| **Invisible** | Heavily obscured for hiding; attacks vs you disadvantage; your attacks advantage (until you hide badly, etc.) |
| **Paralyzed** | Incapacitated, can’t move/speak; auto-fail STR/DEX saves; attacks vs you advantage; hits from within 5 ft are crits |
| **Petrified** | Turned to substance; weight ×10; incapacitated; resistant/immune patterns per SRD; auto-fail STR/DEX saves |
| **Poisoned** | Disadvantage on attacks and ability checks |
| **Prone** | Crawl or stand (costs movement); attacks vs you advantage if within 5 ft else disadvantage; your attacks disadvantage |
| **Restrained** | Speed 0; attacks vs you advantage; your attacks and DEX saves disadvantage |
| **Stunned** | Incapacitated, can’t move; auto-fail STR/DEX saves; attacks vs you advantage |
| **Unconscious** | Incapacitated, drop prone, can’t move/speak; auto-fail STR/DEX saves; attacks vs you advantage; melee within 5 ft crits |

**Exhaustion** (levels 1–6): stacked penalties (disadvantage on checks → speed half → … → death). Finish a long rest to reduce one level (SRD). Track carefully — it’s swingy.

**Concentration:** not a condition, but casting another concentration spell / taking damage / being incapacitated can break it (CON save on damage; DC = 10 or half damage, whichever higher).
