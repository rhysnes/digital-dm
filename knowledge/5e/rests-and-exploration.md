# Rests and exploration

## Rests (SRD)

| Rest | Time | Typical benefits |
|------|------|------------------|
| **Short** | ≥ 1 hour | Spend Hit Dice to heal; some features recharge |
| **Long** | ≥ 8 hours (with constraints) | Regain HP, Hit Dice (half total max ≥ 1), most slots/features; exhaustion −1 level |

Interrupting a long rest can cancel benefits. More than one long rest per 24 hours generally doesn’t stack benefits (SRD timing).

## Travel pace (common SRD table idea)

Slow / normal / fast — trade speed vs Stealth / Notice. Use when overland time matters; otherwise montage.

## Light & vision

- **Bright / dim / darkness** interact with darkvision and obscurement.  
- **Lightly obscured** → disadvantage on Perception (sight).  
- **Heavily obscured** → effectively blinded for sight.

## Food, water, suffocation

Track only when the fiction stresses survival. SRD has depletion rules; “5e loose” may handwave until the desert episode.

## Downtime

Crafting, research, work — high-level public practice: spend gold/time, make checks, advance clocks. Detail as needed per campaign.
